# Star Trek Prolog Storyboard Feature

## 🎬 **Epic Story Introduction**

The Star Trek application now features a cinematic prolog storyboard that introduces players to the epic 12-act campaign "The Horizon Chronicles" when starting a new game.

## 🚀 **How to Experience the Prolog**

1. **Access the Application**: Open the Star Trek dashboard
2. **Look for "New Game" Button**: On the title screen (before login), you'll see a prominent gold "New Game" button
3. **Start the Experience**: Click "New Game" to begin the cinematic prolog
4. **Enjoy the Story**: Experience 20 cinematic scenes introducing the main storyline

## 🎭 **Prolog Features**

### **20 Cinematic Scenes**

- **Opening Titles**: "The Horizon Chronicles - A Star Trek Story"
- **Mission Briefing**: USS Horizon's Delta Quadrant mission
- **Character Introductions**: Meet Captain Nova, Commander Thresh, Dr. Kalar, Lt. Vex
- **Ancient Mystery**: Discovery of prehistoric energy signatures
- **Shadow Awakening**: The mysterious Shadow Emperor stirs
- **Epic Scope**: Preview of the 12-act, 360-episode campaign

### **Interactive Controls**

- **Play/Pause**: Control playback timing
- **Skip Forward**: Jump to next scene
- **Skip All**: Jump directly to the game
- **Audio Toggle**: Mute/unmute sound effects
- **Progress Tracking**: See overall and scene progress

### **Visual Effects**

- **Dynamic Backgrounds**: Stars, nebulae, ship exteriors, shadow realms
- **Cinematic Transitions**: Smooth scene changes with fade effects
- **Character Dialogue**: Immersive dialogue boxes with speaker identification
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Ominous tones, dark whispers, stellar animations

## 📖 **Story Overview**

The prolog introduces the main narrative elements:

### **The USS Horizon Mission**

- Commanded by Captain Elena Nova
- Stationed in the Delta Quadrant
- Mission: Establish Federation presence and explore unknown regions

### **Main Characters**

- **Captain Elena Nova**: Diplomatic leader with tactical brilliance
- **Commander Thresh**: Loyal Andorian first officer
- **Dr. Kalar Voss**: Bajoran medical officer and researcher
- **Lieutenant Vex**: Young Vulcan science officer
- **Chief Korvak**: Klingon security chief

### **The Ancient Threat**

- Discovery of energy signatures older than known civilizations
- Awakening of the Shadow Coalition
- The mysterious Shadow Emperor's return
- Setup for the epic 12-act confrontation

### **Campaign Scope**

- **12 Acts** of story progression
- **30 Episodes** per act (360 total episodes)
- **10 Chapters** per episode (3,600 total chapters)
- Choices matter - diplomatic, combat, and exploration decisions
- Character relationship building
- Multiple civilizations and conflicts

## 🎮 **Integration with Game Systems**

The prolog seamlessly connects to:

- **Story Missions System**: Interactive gameplay based on the narrative
- **Character Creation**: Build relationships with the introduced crew
- **Weapons System**: Utilize the arsenal shown in combat scenarios
- **Game Modes**: Experience the story through various gameplay modes

## 🌟 **User Experience**

- **First-Time Players**: See the full "New Game" experience
- **Returning Players**: Button changes to "Replay Story" after initial viewing
- **Skip Option**: Players can skip the prolog and jump straight to gameplay
- **Immersive Design**: Full-screen cinematic experience with Star Trek LCARS styling

## 🔧 **Technical Features**

- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices
- **Performance Optimized**: Efficient animations and transitions
- **Accessible Controls**: Clear navigation and control options
- **Progress Persistence**: Remembers if user has seen the prolog
