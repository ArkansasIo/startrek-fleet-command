## Audio Implementation Notes

### File Formats

- **Primary**: MP3 (for web compatibility)
- **Fallback**: OGG Vorbis
- **Quality**: 192kbps for music, 128kbps for effects

### Audio Cues for Prolog Scenes

1. **Opening Title**: Main theme begins softly, builds to full orchestration
2. **Character Introductions**: Individual character motifs woven into main theme
3. **Shadow Scenes**: Dissonant undertones, reversed audio effects
4. **Epic Moments**: Full orchestra with choir
5. **Transitions**: Musical bridges between scenes

### Star Trek Musical DNA

Our compositions incorporate classic Star Trek musical elements:

- **Jerry Goldsmith's** harmonic progressions
- **Alexander Courage's** fanfare style
- **Dennis McCarthy's** thematic development
- **Michael Giacchino's** modern orchestration techniques

### Technical Implementation

- **Adaptive Volume**: Music automatically adjusts based on scene intensity
- **Seamless Looping**: Background tracks loop without noticeable breaks
- **Dynamic Mixing**: Multiple audio layers can play simultaneously
- **Effect Triggering**: Sound effects triggered by scene events and user actions
